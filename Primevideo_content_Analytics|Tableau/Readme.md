# 🎬 Prime Video Content Analytics Dashboard

> A Business Intelligence and Data Visualization project leveraging Tableau to analyze Prime Video's content ecosystem, uncover catalog trends, evaluate audience segmentation, and provide actionable insights into content distribution, genre performance, and global streaming patterns.

---

# Executive Summary

The Prime Video Content Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed to explore and analyze Prime Video's content library through advanced data visualization and analytical reporting techniques.

As streaming platforms continue to expand their content portfolios, understanding content composition, audience targeting, release strategies, and geographic distribution has become increasingly important for business decision-making. This project transforms raw Prime Video catalog data into a comprehensive analytical dashboard that enables stakeholders to evaluate content performance, identify trends, and gain deeper visibility into the platform's content strategy.

The dashboard provides insights into content types, ratings, genres, release patterns, and country-level distribution while demonstrating best practices in data storytelling, dashboard design, and business intelligence reporting.

---

# Business Problem

Streaming platforms manage thousands of content titles spanning multiple genres, audience categories, release periods, and geographic regions. Without a structured analytical framework, it becomes difficult to answer critical business questions such as:

* Which genres dominate the platform's content portfolio?
* What proportion of the catalog consists of Movies versus TV Shows?
* How has content production evolved over time?
* Which audience segments are most heavily targeted?
* Which countries contribute the largest share of content?
* What content patterns can inform future acquisition and production strategies?

This project addresses these questions through a centralized analytics dashboard that provides a holistic view of Prime Video's content landscape.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:
