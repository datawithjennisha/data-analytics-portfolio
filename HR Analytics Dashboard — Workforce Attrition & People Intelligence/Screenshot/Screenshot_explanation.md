
### 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard — Workforce Attrition & People Intelligence

This interactive HR Analytics Dashboard was developed in Microsoft Power BI to analyze workforce attrition, employee demographics, salary distribution, job satisfaction, and departmental performance.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of key HR metrics through:

- KPI Cards for Employees, Attrition, Age, and Experience
- Department-wise Attrition Analysis
- Salary Slab Attrition Breakdown
- Job Role & Satisfaction Matrix
- Gender Distribution Analysis
- Experience-based Attrition Trends
- Department Employee Distribution
- Interactive Age Group Filtering

The analysis helped identify high-risk employee groups, attrition drivers, compensation patterns, and workforce trends to support data-driven HR decision-making and retention planning.
